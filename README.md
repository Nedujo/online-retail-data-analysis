# Online Retail Data Analysis

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores an online retail dataset using R to understand product performance, sales quantities, revenue trends, and country-level sales performance.

The aim is to extract meaningful business insights from transactional data and demonstrate practical data analytics skills.

---

## 2. Tools Used

- R
- RStudio
- Microsoft Excel (dataset)
- Data visualisation using R

---

## 3. Project Objectives

- Analyse product sales performance.
- Identify the highest-revenue products.
- Identify the most frequently purchased products.
- Examine monthly revenue trends.
- Compare revenue generated across countries.
- Provide business recommendations based on the findings.

---

## 4. Key Findings

1. **Highest-revenue product:** Regency Cakestand 3 Tier, generating £174,484.74.
2. **Second-highest revenue:** Paper Craft, Little Birdie, generating £168,469.60.
3. **Highest quantity sold:** Paper Craft, Little Birdie, with 80,995 units.
4. **Leading country by revenue:** United Kingdom, generating £7,308,391.55.
5. **Highest monthly revenue:** November 2011, with £1,461,756.20.

---

## 5. Project Visualisations

The project includes five charts:

1. Bottom 10 Products by Quantity Sold
2. Monthly Revenue Trend
3. Top 10 Countries by Revenue
4. Top 10 Products by Quantity Sold
5. Top 10 Products by Revenue

---

## 6. Business Recommendations

- Monitor stock levels for products with high sales quantities.
- Maintain sufficient inventory of high-revenue products.
- Investigate seasonal sales patterns to improve inventory and promotional planning.
- Continue analysing the UK market while identifying opportunities in other countries.
- Use both revenue and quantity sold when evaluating product performance.

---

## 7. Conclusion

The analysis examined product performance, sales quantities, monthly revenue trends, and country-level revenue.

The findings identified the highest-revenue products, the most frequently purchased products, the leading revenue-generating country, and the strongest sales month.

The project demonstrates how data analysis can support inventory planning, seasonal sales preparation, and market-focused business decisions.
